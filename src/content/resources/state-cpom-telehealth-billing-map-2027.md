We built this map for ourselves because Anvil bills in 14 states and we kept re-doing the same research for every new customer. It is the document we wish had existed.

## The six dimensions
1. *CPOM strictness.* Strict, moderate, or permissive, with a note on enforcement activity.
2. *Management fee treatment.* Whether percentage-of-revenue management fees are permitted, restricted, or effectively prohibited under fee-splitting rules.
3. *Physician owner requirements.* Whether the PC owner must be licensed in that state specifically, and what substantive role is expected.
4. *Entity registration.* Whether a foreign PC can register, or a domestic entity is required; any professional entity naming or board approval requirements.
5. *Telehealth billing rules.* Modality (video, audio-only), place-of-service, in-state clinician licensure requirements, any in-state address requirement, and Medicaid telehealth allowances for behavioral health.
6. *Recent movement.* Legislation or enforcement in the last 18 months that changed any of the above, and pending bills to watch.

## Three patterns worth knowing
*Strict does not mean impossible.* The strictest states are where most of the country's largest telehealth companies operate. It means the structure has to be done properly: a domestic PC, a physician owner licensed in that state with a real role, a management agreement that does not touch clinical judgment, and a fee structure that survives fee-splitting review.

*Permissive on ownership, strict on telehealth.* Several states with relaxed ownership rules have tight telehealth modality or in-state licensure rules. Founders who sort states by CPOM alone get surprised by billing rules.

*Movement is toward more scrutiny.* Over the last two years, multiple states have introduced or passed measures increasing review of private-equity and management company involvement in practices, and some have tightened enforcement. The map's "movement" column is the one to check quarterly.

## How to use this
Before forming an entity in a new state: check all six columns. Before contracting in a new state: check column five. Before a fundraise: check column six for every state you operate in, because diligence will.
