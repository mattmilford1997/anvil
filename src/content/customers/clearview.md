## Context

Clearview is a hybrid behavioral health group operated by our team. It had a working payer contract in its home state and a waiting list in two more.

## The problem

Replicating a contract in a new state is a new enrollment, a new panel, and often a new entity. The home-state playbook did not transfer, and the clinicians hired for the new states were waiting.

## What we did

1. New-state clinicians roster-added under the rented PC's contracts, so the waiting list moved in weeks.
2. Clearview's PC foreign-qualified in the new states. Physician owner already in place.
3. Contracts for Clearview's entity filed in each new state. Volume migrated payer by payer as they landed.
